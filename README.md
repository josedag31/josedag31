## About me

I am a third-year Software Engineering student at the **University of Seville**, interested in the point where software meets control, vehicle dynamics and artificial intelligence.

- 🏎️ **Head of Manual Control** in the Vehicle Dynamics Department at [ARUS Andalucía Racing Team](https://www.arusteam.com) (Formula Student). I am responsible for the Manual Control area and I review the rest of the control work.
- 🔧 Within it I also work on **Traction Control**, the **tyre model** and the department's **database**.
- 📐 Hands-on experience with **PID and feedforward control** and **vehicle dynamics**.
- 🧠 I work daily with **AI agents and MCP**, I know **Claude** end to end (Claude Code, agents, skills, MCP), and I am learning **machine learning** and **intelligent control**.
- ⚙️ I build tools and **automations**, and I use AI-assisted **reverse engineering** to understand systems whose source code I do not have.
